What this page is

This is a picture gallery of the GALE-CAST website design. It is not the working website. Every image is a screenshot; nothing here can be clicked to search, forecast or send alerts.

  • What it shows: the current design, live since October 13, 2026: the new Home page (Three-Day Forecast Trend at the top, map with moving wind lines, place card, period button, Top 3 Windiest Days and the climatology chart), Public Readiness with "What is a Gale?", About, the gale alert pop-up, and the loading, error and page-not-found screens.
  • Devices and themes: desktop 1440, laptop 1366, tablet (portrait 820 and landscape 1180), phone 390 and small phone 320, each in light and dark.
  • Data in the pictures: whole pages and close-ups use the real GALE-CAST forecast at capture time. The alert pop-ups and the High, Moderate, calm, loading and error states use sample data. Only GALE-CAST forecasts from 2025 to 2030 appear anywhere; PAGASA's past readings are not shown.
  • How to use it: use the buttons in the dark bar to show one theme or one device. Click any picture to open it full size.
  • The real website: https://gale-cast.vercel.app
  • Design by: the GALE-CAST team, Our Lady of Fatima University. Captured October 13, 2026.
