What this page is

This is a picture gallery of the GALE-CAST website design. It is not the working website. Website views are browser screenshots; device notification examples are clearly labeled illustrations. Nothing here can be clicked to search, forecast or send alerts. It follows the order of the GALE-CAST User Manual (updated October 13, 2026).

  • What it shows: every page, feature, pop-up and message of the current website (live since October 13, 2026): the first-visit questions, the new Home page, the Gale Alert (High, Moderate, both, many areas, TEST ONLY survey alerts), notification settings, Public Readiness, About, offline and error screens, and the menu and footer.
  • Devices and themes: desktop, laptop, tablet (portrait and landscape), phone and small phone, in light and dark. 324 pictures: 127 desktop, 52 tablet, 145 phone.
  • Data in the pictures: pages and close-ups use the real GALE-CAST forecast at capture time. Alert captures identify their source: TEST-MIXED and TEST-MULTI use built-in sample data; August 9, 2026 uses the live GALE-CAST forecast. Notification banners are illustrations. Other pictures marked "sample data" use made-up values. Only GALE-CAST forecasts from 2025 to 2030 appear; PAGASA's past readings are not shown.
  • Device notifications: Android and Windows notification examples are illustrations, paired with actual website alert screenshots. Real operating-system notification banners and the browser's location question are not captured here.
  • How to use it: use the buttons in the dark bar to show one theme or one device. Click any picture to open it full size.
  • The real website: https://gale-cast.vercel.app
  • Design by: the GALE-CAST team, Our Lady of Fatima University. Captured October 13, 2026.
