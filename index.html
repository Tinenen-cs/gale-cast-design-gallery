What this page is

This is a picture gallery of the GALE-CAST website design. It is not the working website. Every image is a screenshot. Nothing here can be clicked to search, forecast or send alerts, and the numbers in the pictures are sample data made for previewing, not real forecasts.

  • What it shows: every page and state of the front-end design, on phone, tablet and desktop, in light and dark themes.
  • How to use it: use the buttons in the dark bar at the top to show one theme or one device. Click any picture to open it full size. Use the links below to jump to a part.
  • Where the real website is: https://gale-cast.vercel.app
  • Design by: the GALE-CAST team, Our Lady of Fatima University. Captured October 4, 2026.
